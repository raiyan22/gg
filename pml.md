## Reading list for the top security picks (applicant must write his own questions)
Read in this order; I read the first two myself (see notes), the rest are title-level.

1. **Mahmoud Alfadel (Calgary): "Dependency-Induced Waste in Continuous Integration"** (FSE 2024, with Weeraddana and McIntosh, then at Waterloo). Read in full. Data: 20,743 dependency-only commits across 1,487 npm projects. Finding: 55.88% of CI build time triggered by dependency updates comes from updates to unused dependencies. Proposes Dep-sCImitar, which skips those builds; in a retrospective test it saves 68.34% of the wasted build time at 94% precision. Notes for the applicant: CI data could be retrieved for only 20.9% of the unused-dependency commits; scope is npm and GitHub Actions; automated update bots are named as a possible amplifier. Overlap with the applicant's WellDev line "benchmarked Snyk, DefectDojo and Renovate to optimize CI/CD pipeline runtime": real, but he must state only what he measured himself.
2. **Alfadel et al., "On the Discoverability of npm Vulnerabilities in Node.js Projects"** (TOSEM 2023) and "On the Threat of npm Vulnerable Dependencies in Node.js Applications" (2020, arXiv 2009.09019; reported 67.9% of apps depend directly on vulnerable libraries). Title and summary level only.
3. **Elissa Redmiles (Georgetown)**: publication list read from https://seclab.cs.georgetown.edu/publications/. Recent: S&P 2026 "I Wonder if These Warnings are Accurate": Security and Privacy Advice in Nine Majority World Countries; NDSS 2026 "The Role of Privacy Guarantees in Voluntary Donation of Private Health Data for Altruistic Goals"; CHI 2026 "Having Confidence in My Confidence Intervals"; USENIX Security 2025 "Analyzing the AI Nudification Application Ecosystem". Her topics are user-centered security and online safety (survey, interview and measurement methods), including sensitive subject matter such as image-based abuse. No supply-chain work seen. The applicant has no human-subject security research yet.
4. **Micah Sherr (Georgetown)**: NDSS 2026 "Mirage: Private, Mobility-Based Routing for Censorship Evasion"; USENIX Security 2025 "Censorship Evasion with Unidentified Protocol Generation"; PETS 2025 "SCIF". Network privacy and censorship, far from the applicant's work.

6. **Leah Findlater (UW HCDE)**: CHI 2025 SPECTRA (personalizable sound recognition for deaf and hard-of-hearing users with interactive ML); AR captioning for deaf and hard-of-hearing users. Summary level only.
7. **Danna Gurari (CU Boulder)**: "Long-Form Answers to Visual Questions from Blind and Low Vision People" (COLM 2024; authors Huh, Xu, Peng, Chen, Murugu, Gurari, Choi, Pavel). Read via arXiv abstract.
